05 / MASS CASUALTY

Mass Casualty Kits

$200 / day

An MCI, or mass-casualty incident, is any event where the number of people needing help outnumbers the rescuers able to treat them. A large event, a serious accident, an attack, or any workplace emergency can turn into one fast. This is a bigger kit built for exactly that: bleeding control sized for a crowd, not just one person.

What's Included

Every kit comes stocked

Bleeding control first, cardiac response too

Every Mass Casualty Kit leads with complete stop-the-bleed contents, including tourniquets, chest seals, combat gauze, and bandages, since that's what saves the most lives in the first minutes of a real mass-casualty response. CPR mask, PPE, and a full AED are included as well.

  • Full stop-the-bleed kit contents (tourniquets, chest seals, gauze, bandages, shears)
  • CPR mask and PPE
  • Emergency blankets
  • Emergency litter, for moving someone to safety
  • Triage tags
  • Simple triage instructions
  • AED

Mass-casualty incidents aren't limited to races and festivals. They happen in schools, offices, and workplaces too, and matter more every year in a world with increasing rates of mass casualty incidents. Mass Casualty Kits bring full-scale response equipment wherever a large group of people gathers, for as long as you need it. Every rental comes with a record of what was provided and inspected, which is useful if your event's insurance ever asks what safety measures were in place.
