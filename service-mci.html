Mass Casualty Kits

$200 / day

An MCI, or mass-casualty incident, is any situation where more people need help than usual, such as a large event, a serious accident, an attack, or any emergency at a workplace. This is a bigger kit built for exactly that: bleeding control and a full AED, sized for a crowd, not just one person.

What's Included

Every kit comes stocked

Cardiac and trauma response, together

Every Mass Casualty Kit includes a full AED, CPR mask, trauma shears, and PPE, plus complete stop-the-bleed contents, including tourniquets, chest seals, combat gauze, and bandages, so the equipment that actually saves lives comes first.

  • Full stop-the-bleed kit contents (tourniquets, chest seals, gauze, bandages, shears)
  • AED, CPR mask, and PPE
  • Triage tags
  • Simple triage instructions

Mass-casualty incidents aren't limited to races and festivals. They happen in schools, offices, and workplaces too, and matter more every year in a world with increasing rates of mass casualty incidents. Mass Casualty Kits bring full-scale response equipment wherever a large group of people gathers, for as long as you need it. Every rental comes with a record of what was provided and inspected, which is useful if your event's insurance ever asks what safety measures were in place.
