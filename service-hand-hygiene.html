Why It Matters

Hand hygiene saves lives too

It doesn't take a cardiac arrest or a mass-casualty incident to matter — consistent hand hygiene remains one of the simplest, most effective ways to stop the spread of infection in shared spaces. It's a small habit with an outsized impact, which is exactly why hand hygiene stations sit alongside AEDs and trauma kits in what we offer, not apart from them. Access to basic preventive care is still access to life-saving care.
