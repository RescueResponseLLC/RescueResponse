Fire Extinguisher Service

$30 / month

Most buildings are already required to have fire extinguishers on-site and inspected every year — that's not optional, it's code. We place the extinguishers, run that required inspection, and replace anything discharged or expired — so it's done, not something you have to schedule yourself. Every visit is documented, giving you a paper trail if your compliance is ever questioned.

What's Included

Every placement comes covered

  • Extinguisher(s) sized to your space
  • Certified annual inspection
  • Compliance tagging
  • Replacement on discharge or expiration

What ongoing service includes

Certified annual inspection and tagging, plus replacement if a unit is discharged or ages out — coordinated for you rather than left as a compliance task you have to remember. Every visit logs the date, what was checked, and what was replaced.

Upfront cost depends on extinguisher type and how many units your space needs — quoted after a quick look at your building.
