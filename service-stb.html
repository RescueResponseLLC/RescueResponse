Stop the Bleed Box

$40 / month

A wall-mounted box with everything needed to stop life-threatening bleeding, whether that's from a serious cut, a workplace accident, or worse, in the first few minutes before paramedics arrive. No install fee, just a small monthly service charge.

What's Included

Everything you need for severe bleeding

A proven kit built around tourniquets, hemostatic gauze, and chest seals, everything needed to manage a life-threatening hemorrhage in the minutes before paramedics arrive.

  • CAT tourniquets
  • Chest seals
  • Emergency bandage
  • Combat gauze
  • Trauma shears
  • Basic gauze

What ongoing service includes

Scheduled inspections, expiration tracking on gauze and dressings, and full replacement of anything used in a real bleeding-control event, so the box is always ready, not just mounted. Every visit is documented, giving you a real record of inspection and maintenance you can point to.

In one study of major limb trauma, casualties who had a tourniquet applied had an 87% survival rate, versus 0% survival for those who needed one but didn't get one. Source: tourniquet survival study, Annals of Surgery.
