Idaho · Accessible Healthcare Equipment

Access shouldn't depend on luck.

RescueRadius was built by working paramedics with one goal: increase easy, affordable public access to emergency equipment. Early defibrillator use in sudden cardiac arrest and early tourniquet application both measurably reduce mortality.

Our Mission

Why this exists

Most life-saving equipment doesn't fail because the technology is inadequate — it fails because it isn't there, or it's there but nobody checked on it. An event without an AED, a trail crew without bleeding control, a business without a naloxone box: these are gaps in access, not gaps in medicine. Closing them, one placement at a time, is the entire reason RescueRadius exists.
