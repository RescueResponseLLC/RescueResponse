This service has a minimum initial term of six (6) months from the installation date ("Minimum Term"). After the Minimum Term, Client may cancel at any time by providing written notice to RescueRadius@outlook.com, effective at the end of the current billing month.

Placing and maintaining this kit involves a real upfront cost that isn't recovered in a single month of service — this fee exists to protect that investment if service ends early, not to penalize genuine circumstances.
