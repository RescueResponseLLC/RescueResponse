Why Monthly Service

Equipment that's actually ready

Owning a first aid box no one has touched in three years isn't preparedness. Every RescueRadius placement is backed by a monthly service plan — scheduled inspections, expired component swaps (batteries, pads, medications, tourniquets), and replacement of anything used or damaged. You get a call when something needs attention, not an expired device when it matters most. It's the difference between having equipment and having equipment you can actually count on.

Hand hygiene saves lives too

It doesn't take a cardiac arrest or a mass-casualty incident to matter — consistent hand hygiene remains one of the simplest, most effective ways to stop the spread of infection in shared spaces. That's why our hand hygiene stations sit alongside AEDs and trauma kits, not apart from them.

Why RescueRadius

Built by paramedics

RescueRadius was built by working paramedics with one goal: increase easy, affordable public access to emergency equipment. Early defibrillator use in sudden cardiac arrest and early tourniquet application both measurably reduce mortality — and that matters more every year, in a world with increasing rates of mass casualty incidents.
