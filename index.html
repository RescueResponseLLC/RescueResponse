We place bleeding control, naloxone, and fire extinguishers into the businesses and public spaces that don't have them, and AEDs and mass casualty kits wherever an event or expedition takes people out of easy reach — inspected, restocked, and ready, not handed over once and forgotten.

Why Ongoing Service

Equipment that's actually ready

A first aid kit that's been on the wall for years without a single check isn't real preparedness — it's a false sense of security. Every RescueRadius placement includes ongoing care: scheduled inspections, expired component swaps (batteries, pads, medications, tourniquets), and replacement of anything used or damaged. It's not just about installing equipment — it's about making sure it actually works the moment someone needs it.

The Best Equipment

We only carry what we'd trust in the field

We work closely with manufacturers like North American Rescue and ZOLL — not because they sponsor us, but because their equipment is what we'd choose for our own crews and our own families. If we wouldn't stake a real emergency on it, it doesn't go on the wall.
