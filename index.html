Worth Thinking About

The question nobody asks

Cardiac arrest survival drops roughly 10% every minute without a defibrillator. If someone was hurt today, on your property, at your event, or on your crew, would the equipment on hand actually work? Would you be able to prove it?

Most places aren't required to own this equipment. But gear that's installed and forgotten can be worse than having none, and a few dollars a month is far cheaper than the alternative: no record, no proof, nothing to show you were prepared.

That's what RescueRadius actually provides: not just functional equipment, but a documented record. Inspection dates, what was checked, what was replaced. Something real to hand an insurer, an inspector, or your own legal counsel. Proof, not a guess.
